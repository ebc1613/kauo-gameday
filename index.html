About the datasources, limits, definitions

Built with AI. This prototype was made with help from AI (Claude). The main numbers were checked against the raw data, but mistakes are possible. Use it as a planning aid, not an official count.

Where the counts come from. AUO had no control tower before 2024, so the FAA has no official daily counts for these seasons. Counts come from ADS-B tracking (a nearby receiver plus the adsb.lol network) and anything dispatch enters by hand. Coverage starts September 13, 2024. Aircraft without ADS-B Out, or too low for nearby receivers, are missed, so treat the numbers as a floor and use them to compare weekends.

This data source is temporary. The ADS-B receiver that sees traffic down to the runway is privately owned and will be leaving Auburn. After that, new weekends will have far fewer (or no) counts, and the page will say so at the top. To keep this going, the fix is an ADS-B receiver on the field itself: under $350 all in.

Movement means one landing or one takeoff, the way a tower counts operations. Comparisons use arrivals and departures (aircraft coming from or going somewhere else), because Auburn Aviation pattern work would drown out the game-day effect. Auburn Aviation means AUB callsigns, aircraft that have flown as AUB, and the university-registered 172s and Seminoles. Normal is the same day averaged over that season's away and bye weekends.

Stadium TFR shading shows the standing stadium flight restriction for Division I football (3 NM around Jordan-Hare, up to 3,000 ft AGL, from 1 hour before kickoff until 1 hour after the game ends). KAUO sits just inside that ring. Times here are estimates: always check NOTAMs.

Weather tags come from KAUO's own METARs (Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive): the flight category that held for most of the daytime (7 am to 7 pm), plus rain, thunderstorms and gusts.

Live board comes from planedata.io's free KAUO board (its own feeders plus adsb.lol, ODbL, and The OpenSky Network), loaded in your browser every minute. Only aircraft types and origins are shown here, never tail numbers or callsigns. If planedata can't be reached, a backup snapshot from adsb.lol shows on game weekends.
